# Sa Pamboleria — research brief

Inca, Mallorca · 2026-09-20 · sources: Google Places, Instagram, Facebook, web, owner assets

## Who they are

A family-run Mallorcan restaurant in the Cristo Rey neighbourhood of Inca. Two things carry the name:
*pa amb oli* — thirteen versions of it, and the reason they are called "Sa Pamboleria" — and *carns a la
brasa*, charcoal-grilled meat now sold on Instagram as "Carnes Maduradas Premium". Two covered terraces,
free parking, 721 Google reviews at 4.3. The customers write in Catalan and talk about portions, not
presentation. Deep local following, no website.

## Services & offer

- **Pa amb oli**, 13 versions, €10–16 — cured cheese, Iberian ham, cuttlefish with sobrassada, lamb chops
- **A la brasa**, 13 cuts, €12–24 — sirloin, chuletón, entrecôte, Iberian pluma, quail, rabbit, poussin
- **Para picar** €2–19 · **specialities** €14–26 (lobster, oxtail, house parrillada, "Mar, Tierra y Aire")
- **Fish** €15–21 · **salads** €9–12 · **kids'** €8 · **four homemade cakes** €6.50
- Morning *berenar* service (8:30 opening Thu–Sun); dine-in, takeaway, free parking

Full menu — 8 sections, 57 dishes with prices — transcribed in `.studio/sources/menu.json`.

## Reputation

**4.3 / 5 from 721 Google reviews.** Two older snapshots (677 and 624, same 4.3) show steady growth, not a
conflict. Praised for grilled meat and cuttlefish, abundant portions, attentive service, cleanliness,
parking and the homemade desserts. Criticised for a slow kitchen when busy.

One outlier: a stale **TripAdvisor listing at 2.6/5 from 11 reviews**. Eleven against 721 — Google is the
real figure, don't publish the TripAdvisor number, but the owner should know it exists.

## Tone of voice

Warm, unpretentious, family-run. Their own writing is short, exclamatory and emoji-led — an invitation
rather than a pitch. Customers are generous and specific. Traits: **warm · unpretentious · generous ·
local-first · direct · proud of the produce.**

The language split should shape the build: the **logo is Catalan**, the **printed menu entirely Spanish**,
**Instagram Spanish**, **Google reviews Catalan**. Treat Catalan as the brand's own voice and Spanish as
the operational one. Dish names stay Catalan in every language.

## Visual identity today

No brand system — a printed menu, and a logo that exists nowhere as a file.

**Logo**: "Sa Pamboleria" hand-lettered in brush script, navy on white, sprout above the "o", with
"TERRASSA · PA AMB OLI" curved above and "CARNS A LA BRASA" below. Survives **only as printed artwork
inside the menu photographs** — ~350–400px wide at best. No vector, no transparent PNG, no brand guide.
**Menu design**: serif headings, bold sans dish names, watercolour olive branch, vegetables along the foot
— warm rustic-Mediterranean. **Colours** navy, white, pale sage, read off a colour-cast photo: indicative
only.

**Images are the real constraint.** 31 pulled from social, **22 usable**, all phone-shot at **1080px wide**
(1170 max). Food shots are honest and appetising but cropped hard to the frame edge and flat-lit. Eight
unusable: a 320px avatar, a customer's face, six Uber Eats reposts with visible watermark. Best: `ig-12`
(abundant flat lay), `ig-14` (charcuterie on wood), `ig-09` (cured meat on black slate, the only real
contrast), `ig-02` (terrace, promo text baked in).

**The owner's hero idea cannot be built from these.** A cut-out pa amb oli with a hand reaching in needs
clean separation and resolution to spare; all four pa amb oli shots are edge-cropped with the plate rim cut
off, and no hand-reaching shot exists. **Decided 2026-09-20:** the hero is a full-bleed crop of `ig-12`
(alternate `ig-14`) — the honest free route, and it suits a restaurant whose customers praise abundance.
The cut-out treatment stays available if a shoot or paid generation is funded later.

## GAPS & LOW-CONFIDENCE

**Decided 2026-09-20 — carried as assumptions, not resolved**

- **Image rights — assumed granted so design can proceed.** Every site photo comes from scraped social. The
  2021 run was shot by a credited hired photographer (@alv_visuals), six are Uber Eats reposts, one is a
  reposted creator video. The owner will confirm later what may be used. **This unblocks design; it does not
  unblock launch** — rights must be settled before the site goes public.
- **Hero — full-bleed crop of `ig-12`** (alternate `ig-14`), not the cut-out. Reversible if a shoot or paid
  generation is funded later.

Eight images stay out **on quality grounds, independent of rights**: a 320px avatar, a low-res video cover
with heavy text over an identifiable customer, and six reposts with an Uber Eats watermark burned into the
frame. That leaves **22 usable images**.

**Blocking**

- **Logo** — printed inside a photo only. → Trace/upscale `menu-page-1.png`, **or ask whether the menu's
  designer still has the source file** — cheapest and best, and not yet asked.
- **Opening hours** — Google and TripAdvisor disagree on closing days *and* times. Wrong restaurant hours
  cause real harm. → Owner confirms, including whether the 8:30 *berenar* still runs.
- **Menu currency** — photographed menu is undated, and the bio's dry-aged "Carnes Maduradas Premium" isn't
  on it. → Owner confirms prices and supplies anything missing.

**Useful** — Catalan/English menu translations (owner should check the Catalan); no founding date, owner
names or origin story, so "about" has nothing factual to stand on; no email or booking system; the Places
API caps at **5 reviews of 721**, four of them 2023 or earlier; only 12 of 128 Instagram posts returned.

**Minor** — "De Baicon queso" and "Hamburgesa Angus" appear as printed: confirm, don't silently correct;
no press or awards (normal at this size — don't manufacture an "as featured in"); TripAdvisor listing worth
claiming; crawl skipped because no site exists.

**Low confidence** — second phone number (one 2021 post); cocktail terrace (2021 only); €10 *berenar* (one
undated aggregator); delivery (listed, unconfirmed); menu colours (colour-cast photo); the tagline (a logo
lockup read as a site tagline).
