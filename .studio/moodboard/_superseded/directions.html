Sa Pamboleria — 3 directions

One page, ca/es/en, with the restyled menu opening in-page as an overlay. The client is named after pa amb oli and sells charcoal-grilled meat from a thatched terrace in Inca; they have 721 Google reviews, no website, no vector logo, and 22 usable phone photographs. Each direction below bets on a different part of that: the fire, the menu, or the place.

Typefaces were read off four real sites with a computed-style probe (see board.md), colours were sampled from the client's own photographs, and every direction was run through the design registry. Nothing here is chosen by elimination. Highlighted items are [CONFIRM: …] placeholders — facts the brief lists as unresolved, carried into the design rather than used as a reason to cut a section.

1 · Sa Brasa (ember-brasa-premium)

The dry-aged meat, cut out of its plate and floated on oxblood — the colour of the beef itself, not the copper-and-flame the sector defaults to. This is the direction that delivers the owner's product-showcase hero, and the only one that does.

Inca, Mallorca

Carns a la brasa

“la carn feta a la brasa està molt bona i la sípia també”

— real Google review, Catalan. Newsreader 400 / Hanken Grotesk 400.

Every swatch, measured. Salt on oxblood 13.56:1 · ember on oxblood 4.66:1 · salt on char 16.23:1 · aged on oxblood 3.61:1 (rules and hover states only, never text — clears the 3:1 non-text floor). The dominant is the burgundy measured in the dry-aged beef taken down to page weight — not a tinted near-black standing in for a colour decision.

Type

Display: Newsreader 200/400 (Google Fonts, variable, optical size) · Body: Hanken Grotesk 400/500/700 (Google Fonts).
Straight from the measured evidence: Etxebarri — the best charcoal-grill site there is — runs Canela at 67px in weight 100 over Untitled Sans at 17px. A large, light high-contrast serif over a quiet grotesque is what premium grill looks like when it isn't shouting. Newsreader is the closest free equivalent with real light weights and optical sizing; Hanken Grotesk is the neutral, slightly warm text face. The competitor's answer to the same problem was Poppins at 20px.

Hero layout

plateless food cut-out, floating, full colour
salt flakes scattered around it
“Carns a la brasa · Pa amb oli” — Newsreader 200, very large
“Tretze talls, de 12 a 24 €.” Opens la carta.

No headline-over-photo. The food is an object on a field, which is the one treatment phone photography carries as well as studio photography does.

Signature motion

The plating. On load the cut-out drops a few pixels and settles, and the salt flakes land around it a beat later, staggered — the gesture of a plate being put down and finished with salt. Once, on the hero, nowhere else. Reduced motion: final state rendered immediately, salt already landed, no movement. Transform and opacity only. (Demo is CSS; the build uses GSAP with matchMedia.)

Honest risk, stated up front. This direction leads on grilled meat, and Cas Teuler's hero says "CARNES A LA BRASA" in the same words. Sampling their capture corrects one thing board.md first got wrong — their page is 38.3% white, not dark — but it makes the real problem clearer: the clash is the proposition, not the palette. Sa Pamboleria would be the second Mallorcan grill restaurant saying the same sentence, with worse photography. What differs is the treatment (plateless cut-outs, a real type scale, oxblood) and the fact that they also have the bread and the terrace — but the opening claim is shared.

Hero decision, on the record. brief.md recorded on 2026-09-20 that the hero would be a full-bleed crop of ig-12, not the cut-out. The owner reversed that in this session, asking to enhance ig-11 and revive the product-showcase idea. That reversal was then tested, not assumed: the enhancement cost €0.0024 and worked, and the cut-out works as food without its plate. This direction is built on the owner's reversal, and the brief will be reconciled at the design stage.

2 · La Carta (navy-menu-typographic)

The site is the menu. Built in the client's own navy — the ink sampled from their printed wordmark — around the one thing the brief calls a must-have. The only direction whose dominant colour the client already owns, and the only one that declines to open on a photograph.

Terrassa · Pa amb oli · Carns a la brasa

Pa amb oli

“Vàrem gaudir d'un deliciós pa amb oli de presa ibèrica, sípia, cuixot salat, etc.”

— real Google review, Catalan. Bricolage Grotesque 800 / Source Serif 4 400.

Every swatch, measured. Paper on navy 9.16:1 · oli on navy 5.28:1 (prices sit here) · navy on paper 9.16:1 · tomàquet on paper 4.63:1. Tomàquet is never used on the navy — it measures 1.98:1 there, so it is confined to the cream menu card where it passes. Olivera appears only inside the line illustration, carrying no text.
The navy is #2E4162, recovered from the printed wordmark by white-balancing the menu photograph against the paper's own white point — the photo was so blue-cast that white paper read as #A6B9EE. This is the only real brand colour the client has.

Type

Display: Bricolage Grotesque 400/700/800 (Google Fonts, variable, optical size) · Dish names & body: Source Serif 4 400/600 (Google Fonts).
Two measured patterns combined. Tacos My Guey — the owner's own reference — runs a single display face at 74px doing all the structural work; Da Maria, their lead reference, sets a slab serif (Bitter) for body because a menu's dish names want a serif. Bricolage supplies the structure and the scale without being one of the grotesques already in the registry; Source Serif carries 57 Catalan dish names at small size without tiring. Neither is a script — the client's brush wordmark must be the only handwritten thing on the page.

The trade-off this direction makes, stated rather than hidden. The brief lists "a product-showcase hero" among the owner's must-haves, and this direction does not provide one — it opens on type and the wordmark instead. The argument for doing that: the photography is flat-lit phone work at 1080px and every other restaurant in the sector opens on a food photograph, so declining to is the strongest available move. The argument against: it is also the convenient move, and it makes the weakest asset in the pack — a 350–400px wordmark recovered from a photograph — the largest object on the page. That wordmark must be traced to clean vector before this direction can be built. If the owner wants their hero idea, direction 1 is where it is.

3 · Sa Terrassa (olive-terrace-daylight)

Daylight, thatch and olive wood — the covered terrace customers actually describe, and the one thing the competitor has no photograph of. The only light direction in the set, and the only one buildable today with no generated or retouched asset beyond one cleanup.

Carrer de Miquel dels Sants Oliver, 6 · Inca

Sa terrassa

“Un lloc on és fàcil aparcar i les instal·lacions estan molt netes.”

— real Google review, Catalan. Fraunces 300 / Karla 400.

Palette — sampled from ig-02

Grava #E8DCC4

Olivera #4E5C28
accent, rules
Ombra #2A2A20

Fusta #63512F

Every swatch, measured. Ombra on grava 10.66:1 · olivera on grava 5.36:1 · fusta on grava 5.63:1 · navy on grava 7.55:1. The "letterpress" tonal list is fusta #63512F on grava #E8DCC4 — 5.63:1, named explicitly because a tone-on-tone list is exactly where contrast quietly fails. The wood tone was darkened from the sampled #735F41 (4.49:1, under AA) for that reason.
Sampled directly: gravel #846B49, cream wall #DBC090, foliage #6B7635, olive wood #735F41. Deliberately not the cream-and-terracotta default — the accent is the olive green measured in the client's own planting, and the field is 54–65 RGB from every cream in the registry.

Type

Display: Fraunces 300/400/600 (Google Fonts, variable — optical size, SOFT and WONK axes) · Body: Karla 400/500/700 (Google Fonts).
Da Maria — the owner's lead reference — sets Playfair Display at 244.8px: the move is a high-contrast display serif allowed to be enormous. Fraunces does that with optical sizing built for it, and its soft/wonk axes give a warmth that suits a family restaurant where Playfair would read as a wine bar. Karla is the plain, slightly quirky grotesque underneath. Neither is on the ban list or in the registry.

Hero layout

navy script wordmark, small, left · lang switcher right
terrace photograph, matted inside a generous oat margin
(a print on a wall, not a full-bleed banner)
annotated: parking · covered terrace · berenar tables
“Sa terrassa” — Fraunces 300, very large, breaking the mat
“Hi ha aparcament gratuït i no cal reservar.”

The photo is matted rather than bled, which is what makes a 1080px phone shot look deliberate instead of stretched. The headline overlaps the mat — the one grid-break. The hero and the terrace annotations are one section, not two, so the only usable terrace photograph is used once.

Signature motion

Light through the thatch. On load a soft warm gradient sweeps once across the terrace photograph, left to right — the sun moving through the palm-frond parasols. Under three seconds, never repeats. Reduced motion: the sweep layer is removed entirely and the photograph shows unmodified — no permanent gradient wash left sitting over the hero as decoration.

Imagery — 14 of 22 usable files, the broadest use in the set

Warm and bright throughout, with a single dark counterpoint: the aged beef on slate is the only dark band on the page, and that value contrast is the drama — spent once, not everywhere.

  • ig-02 terrace — hero, matted, used once (wide)
  • ig-09 aged beef on slate — the single dark band (detail)
  • ig-12, ig-14 — the abundance customers praise (flat lay, board)
  • ig-30, ig-20, ig-10 — the thirteen (object)
  • ig-15, ig-17 peix · ig-29 oxtail · ig-05 ensalades · ig-21, ig-13, ig-04 — breadth beyond the easy files
  • Not used: ig-03, ig-06–ig-08, ig-16, ig-19, ig-22, fb-01 (duplicate of ig-02). No dessert photograph exists.

Known retouch cost. ig-02 has the script wordmark baked into it as a large white overlay, and fb-01 is the same frame. Since this direction makes that photograph the hero, the overlay has to be painted out at media stage — not a crop, a retouch — or the page shows the logo twice. This is the one asset cost this direction carries.
